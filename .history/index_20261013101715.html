Guided electronics projects

Isolated low-voltage DC only
01LED status indicatorResistor sizing · polarity · safe current

Parts: 5 V current-limited supply, LED, series resistor, breadboard and leads.

  1. Use the LED worksheet to select a resistor from the forward voltage and target current.
  2. With power off, wire supply positive → resistor → LED anode; return the cathode to supply negative.
  3. Check polarity and resistor path before applying power.
  4. Compare the model current with the expected value; power down before reversing the LED.

Expected: a protected LED lights at modest current; reversed polarity leaves it off.

02RC timing monitorTime constant · capacitor behavior

Parts: low-voltage supply, series resistor, capacitor with suitable voltage rating, and a high-impedance meter.

  1. Choose R and C, then calculate τ = R × C in the RC worksheet.
  2. Draw the series charging path and confirm capacitor polarity if electrolytic.
  3. Ask an instructor to review the diagram before powering the current-limited supply.
  4. Record capacitor voltage over time and compare the trend with the approximate 5τ settling estimate.

Expected: capacitor voltage rises gradually toward the supply; real timing varies with tolerances and loading.

03Transistor load switchControl signal · load path · datasheet limits

Parts: low-voltage NPN transistor, current-limited supply, small indicator load, base resistor and schematic.

  1. Identify the transistor pinout and ratings from its exact datasheet.
  2. Calculate the load and base-drive requirements; choose the base resistor from verified device data.
  3. With power removed, build the low-side switch and share the circuit reference/return.
  4. Check wiring and current limits with an instructor, then compare off/on behavior at the control input.

Expected: the load follows the control signal without exceeding device, resistor or supply ratings.

These are supervised learning activities, not construction instructions for mains or industrial equipment. Check datasheets and get competent review before energizing a prototype.
